Who We Are

A one-product research site, not the brand's own storefront.

MyGelagen.org exists for one reason: to lay out what's actually verifiable about the Gelagen gummy before you spend money on it. We're not the manufacturer, we don't run the brand's official store, and orders placed through this site go through the brand's own checkout, not ours.

Most product pages you'll find are written by the seller, understandably optimized to get you to buy. We wanted a version that separates "what the brand says" from "what we could confirm ourselves" — so that's roughly the structure of every page on this site.

How we approach the research

  • We report the brand's own stated claims and note plainly where we couldn't independently confirm something, like an exact per-ingredient dosage.
  • We don't repeat medical claims the brand hasn't made itself, and this site's statements haven't been reviewed by the FDA.
  • Reader feedback shown here reflects individual opinions submitted to us, not a controlled study — outcomes vary person to person.
  • Where it's useful, we link out to neutral background sources (like the NIH's Office of Dietary Supplements) for the individual nutrients, separate from any claim about the finished product.

Money and motives

If you buy Gelagen through a link on this site, we may get a commission. It costs you nothing extra, and it doesn't change what we've written — see the full disclaimer for the details.
