The Basics

Where Gelagen fits into a beauty routine

This isn't a cream or a serum — it's a supplement you eat, aimed at adults who want a nutritional angle on skin hydration, hair strength and nail resilience alongside whatever's already in their skincare cabinet. The brand is upfront that it's meant to sit next to your routine, not take it over.

Split the five ingredients into function: one for structure (Biotin), two for cell turnover (B12, Folate), one for oil control (B6), and one for hydration (Pantothenic Acid).

The Formula

Every Gelagen Ingredient, Broken Down

Five named nutrients, each with a stated job to do.

1

Biotin — Structure

Feeds keratin production directly, the protein that gives skin, hair and nails their structural integrity — the goal being firmer nails, tougher hair and smoother-feeling skin.

2

Vitamin B12 (methylcobalamin) — Renewal

An easily absorbed B12 form tied to producing new skin cells — the pitch is quicker recovery from minor blemishes and a more even tone.

3

Vitamin B6 (pyridoxine HCl) — Oil control

Works as a metabolic regulator aimed at keeping oil output in check, particularly in the T-zone.

4

Folate — Renewal

Feeds the same cellular turnover process as B12, swapping in fresh tissue as older cells cycle out.

5

Pantothenic Acid (d-calcium pantothenate) — Hydration

Supports lipid production that keeps the skin's moisture barrier doing its job — the aim being plumper, less dry-looking skin.

Still an open question: we went looking for a per-ingredient dosage number and came up empty across every page we checked. That makes it tough to compare this formula against published research amounts — keep that in mind before you decide.
